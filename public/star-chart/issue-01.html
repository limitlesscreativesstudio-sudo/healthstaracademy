Shift Notes

Your first 90 days on the floor

Three habits that separate the CNAs who thrive from the ones who burn out:

1 Learn names fast. Residents and staff. Nothing builds trust on a unit quicker, and trust is what gets you the good shifts.
2 Chart like someone will read it. Because someone will. Clear, timely documentation protects your residents and your certification.
3 Guard your body. Use the lift equipment every time, even when you're rushed. A back injury in year one can end a career in year one.
Renewal reminder. California CNA certificates renew every two years. You need 48 hours of in service or CEUs, at least 12 each year, and up to 24 of them can be online through CDPH approved providers. You can send your renewal up to 6 months before your expiration date. Don't let yours lapse. CDPH renewal tips

The Long Shift  •  Your money

Read your benefits packet before you sign it

A new job means a stack of benefits paperwork, and most people sign it in the break room without reading it. Look for two things:

The retirement match. If your employer matches contributions, that is part of your pay. Find out what it takes to get all of it.

The life insurance line. Many employers include a small group policy. Know what yours covers, and know that it usually ends when the job does.
